The Inequalities

Budget: 30000x + 50000y ≤ 200000
spend no more than $200k

Energy: 1100x + 1500y ≥ 6000
make at least 6,000 kWh each month

Also: x ≥ 0, y ≥ 0, whole numbers only

Summary + Conclusion

Whitefish needs at least 6,000 kWh a month and cannot spend more than $200,000.

The inequalities show every mix that works. Point (5, 1) is the best: 5 solar and 1 geothermal.

That mix hits the budget, beats the energy goal by 1,000 kWh, keeps yearly upkeep around $4,500, and cuts emissions way below coal power.

Town Hall line: This plan gives Whitefish clean power for winter and summer without going over budget.
